The Issue write gate refuses a `Traps to avoid`, `Stop-and-escalate` or `## Stop conditions` sentence that forbids, or stops on, a change to a path outside the Surface — such a change is published and recorded in the beyond-Surface block, never refused. It also refuses an Issue that declares `Depends-on` and relies on a backticked identifier or string literal no default-branch file contains, unless an `after #<n>:` premise on a declared dependency pins it.
