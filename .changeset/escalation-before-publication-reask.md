A Developer's ruling request or blocker now reaches the Principal even when the turn left work unpublished. The driver reads a `needs_ruling` or `blocked` result before the publication check, so it no longer asks that turn to publish and no longer pauses as infrastructure. It pauses for the Principal with the Developer's own question or blocker, keeps the unpublished work as it is, and names the branch, the changed files and the commits ahead of the remote. A `completed` turn is published as before.
