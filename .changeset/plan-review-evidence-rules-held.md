The Plan reviewer is held to the evidence rules its round validates while it writes. Its output schema now requires at least one piece of evidence on every answer and every finding, and every round's prompt states the two evidence forms (a code location with a path, the captured revision and a line range inside the file; a scope passage copied verbatim from one agreed-scope item). A first round's prompt also says no finding is asked to be confirmed and the confirmations list stays empty.
