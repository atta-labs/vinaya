A push after a default-branch merge keeps its narrowed rerun: when the newest first-parent merge has the recorded head as its first parent and is a clean union of its two sides, the push builds on the merge commit instead of the merge base, even when the merge changed the test inventory, provided every recorded test keeps its suite, runner, working folder, preload and environment. Every other shape keeps the wider selection.
