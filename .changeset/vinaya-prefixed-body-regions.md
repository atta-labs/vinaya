Pull-request body regions are now written with the `VINAYA` marker prefix and the archive's provenance comment with the `### Vinaya provenance` heading. Every reader still accepts the earlier spelling of both, so closed pull requests and in-flight bodies keep parsing the same, and a body regenerated by `vinaya pr report` converts an earlier region in place instead of adding a second one. The region functions take the accepted prefixes from their caller, and the earlier spellings live in one module. The generated workflows wait for a body's Closes region by its field name, whatever its prefix.
